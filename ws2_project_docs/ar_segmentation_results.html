03 — Diagnosis

04

Three causes.
All of them the setup,
none of them the model.

01

The quietest images

A naive date cap picked mid-January 2011: 0.101 % coverage, 987 background pixels per AR pixel. Fix: select the solar maximum, 2013–2015.

02

No positive weight

“Predict nothing” cost 0.24. With a measured pos-weight and a Dice term, the same prediction costs 8.4.

03

LR 10× too high

The config’s own comment said 1e-4; the notebook passed 1e-3. Learning rate is now chosen per model.

04 — Medium budget

05

60 images:
honest, and not enough.

Two seeds, bootstrap intervals, a calibrated threshold. Both seeds hit their best epoch at epoch 1 of 10 and then overfit — the lesson was more images, not more epochs.

The honest report

Training randomness and sampling uncertainty are the same size at this budget.

So the result is the pair (0.459, 0.417) — not one number to three decimals.

08 — Where that leaves us

09

At 60 images, model error
dominated. At 400, it is
the size of the label noise.

Below the floor, more data still buys accuracy. At it, further gains in pixel overlap cannot be told apart from a different choice of threshold in the labelling rule — which is the signal to change the question, not the model.
